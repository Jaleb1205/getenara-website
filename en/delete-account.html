Delete your account and data

For the app Enara by Jakov Brekalo (developer name: Enara) · Version: 29 September 2026

You can delete your account and all of its data yourself at any time, right in the app and immediately. If you can no longer get into the app, we do it for you by email.

1. Delete your account in the app (immediate)

In the app, open the Me tab, go to Settings and scroll all the way down to the section Delete your data. Tap “Delete account for good” and confirm the prompt. Your account and all related data are deleted immediately, with no waiting period, on your device and on our server. This cannot be undone.

If you only want to delete your content and keep the account, choose “Start over (delete everything)” in the same place. That deletes journal entries, ritual answers, profile, conversations, photos, voice recordings and voice messages, while your access stays in place.

2. Deletion by email, if you can no longer get into the app

If you no longer have the app or cannot sign in anymore, write to support@getenara.app with the subject “Delete account”, ideally from the email address you registered with. We delete your account and all related data promptly, within 30 days at the latest, and confirm the deletion to you.

What gets deleted

Both ways delete the same data:

  • Your account (email address, sign in details and the link to Google or Apple)
  • Your journal entries, ritual answers and conversations with Enara
  • Your profile (name, date of birth, wish) and the settings stored in the app
  • Your photos on the Vision Board
  • Your own voice recordings (“In your own voice”)
  • The voice messages from your future self, including their texts
  • The subscription status and free days stored in your account

What is not deleted

Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription. You do that in the settings of your App Store or Google Play account, ideally before deleting. Purchase receipts and payment data are held by Apple or Google under their own responsibility; we never receive them. On our side we only keep data where the law requires it, for example tax records. Such data is deleted once the statutory periods have passed. The details are in our privacy policy.

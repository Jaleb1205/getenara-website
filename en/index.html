Honest

What Enara never promises.

A journal only works if you can be honest in it. The same goes for us.

  • No outcome. Enara never says where your path leads. She helps you show up, a few minutes a day. The path is yours to walk.
  • No amount, no date, no higher power that delivers for you. If your wish is about money, Enara talks about calm and order in your everyday life, never about numbers.
  • No therapy. Enara is an AI and says so openly. In a crisis she points you to the 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline in the US, and to findahelpline.com anywhere else.
  • No invented voices. Maya is a sample account. There are no testimonials on this page, because we won’t make them up.

Questions

Answered briefly.

What does Enara cost?

The rituals, the journal, your sky, the whole library and everything to listen to are free forever. Enara Plus is $12.99 a month, or $59.99 a year billed once, which works out to about $5 a month. That includes conversations with memory, the personal task, messages from your future self, every guided session, greetings and nudges, the letter about your month, talk instead of typing, the moon ritual and all writing templates. Every new account starts with seven free days and full access, no card needed.

Does a subscription start automatically after the free days?

No, never. When the free days end, Enara switches back to the free version on her own. A subscription only exists if you start one yourself in the App Store or on Google Play.

Is Maya real?

No. Maya is a sample account we set up for this page, with a wish in her words and journal entries we wrote. Everything Enara says back to her was generated inside the app. Enara writes something new every time. The screenshots show one real session. She is not a customer, and nothing here is a testimonial from her.

What happens to my entries?

Your entries are stored in your account on servers in Frankfurt, Germany, and sent over an encrypted connection, so they follow you to a new phone. Enara shows no ads and sells no data. You can delete your account and everything in it yourself, right in the app. The details are in the privacy policy.

Does an AI read my journal without asking?

Not unless you say yes. After that, the app sends along a package of context from your account: your profile and your wish, your ritual answers from the last few days, Enara’s own weekly summaries and, as long as you leave the setting “Let Enara read what I write” on, the pages you write on your own. More on that in the privacy policy. Withdraw that consent and Enara stays fully usable as a journal.

Does Enara replace therapy?

No. Enara is a tool for reflection and well-being, not a medical or therapeutic service. In a crisis, Enara points you to the 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline in the US, and to findahelpline.com anywhere else.

Is Enara available for Android?

Yes. Enara is on the App Store for iPhone and on Google Play for Android, in English and German. Your account moves with you from one device to the other.
